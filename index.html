Before you start

  1. One person creates a room and shares the code or QR code. Everyone else joins from their own phone or computer.
  2. The host picks the board size, round length, and difficulty, then starts the round. Solo play works; others can still join the next round.
  3. Everyone sees the same board. Drag through letters on your own screen.
  4. Letters must touch — including diagonals. The trail can turn. You cannot reuse a cell. Most English words of 3–12 letters count.
  5. A unique word scores its length times itself (a 5-letter unique word is 25 points). A word two or more players found is worth 1 point per letter.
  6. When time runs out, the highest point total wins. Ties break on unique finds, then word count.
